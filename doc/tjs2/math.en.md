# Math Class

## Math class

Almost compatible with JavaScript's Math.



Implemented methods:

abs (absolute value)

acos (arc cosine)

asin (arc sine)

atan (arc tangent)

atan2 (two-argument arc tangent)

ceil (round up)

floor (round down)

exp (*e* to the power of n)

log (natural logarithm)

max (the larger of two values)

min (the smaller of two values)

random (random number from 0 up to, but not including, 1)

pow (power)

round (round to the nearest integer)

sin (sine)

cos (cosine)

tan (tangent)

sqrt (square root)



Implemented properties:

E (*e*)

LN10 (log(10))

LN2 (log(2))

LOG10E (base-10 logarithm of *e*)

LOG2E (base-2 logarithm of *e*)

PI (π)

SQRT1_2 (sqrt(1/2))

SQRT2 (sqrt(2))
